# 0003: What's on the Table? Food types

**Date**: 2026-10-11
**Lesson**: lessons/0004-whats-on-the-table-food-types.html
**Duration**: ~30 minutes (target)

## What we covered

A food-types-only lesson, at the learner's request: the dishes on a Bangladeshi
family table grouped into five "plates" (rice & bread, fish/meat/egg, dal & veg,
sweets & fruit, drinks & the table), plus three small frames to use them
(Eta ki? / Ami ___ khub pochondo kori / Apni ki pochondo koren?).
Backbone technique: **spaced review**. Three months since the last session, so
the warm-up revisits Lesson 2 (cha khaben, shundor vs mishti, ami bhoraa)
before any new words. Practice is a retrieval sort game (Bangla word only,
meaning shown after choosing) plus an interleaved quiz with L2/L3 items.

## Family feedback applied this session

No new feedback since 0002. Pani (not jol) carried forward as a constraint:
it appears in the Drinks plate with a "Her family says" callout and is
drilled again in the quiz (jhal → pani deben?).

## What the learner already knew

Eight food words were already in the glossary (bhaat, roti, mach, mangsho,
dal, torkari, mishti, cha, pani) but had never been taught as a set. They are
marked "review" in the lesson.
