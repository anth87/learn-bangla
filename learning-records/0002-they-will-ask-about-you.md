# 0002: They're Asking About You — answering questions about yourself

**Date**: 2026-07-08
**Lesson**: lessons/0003-they-will-ask-about-you.html
**Duration**: ~30 minutes (target)

## What we covered

Answering the four questions that always come once tea is poured: how's work,
what do you do, are you learning Bangla, how's your health. Plus returning the
question with "ar apni?" / "ar tumi?" — the register switch in live use.
Backbone technique: **interleaving** — the quiz deliberately mixes Lesson 1–2
material (aro neen, family forms) with new self-talk phrases.

## Family feedback applied this session

- **Ammu** (not Amma) — the wife calls her mum Ammu; lesson 1 card updated
  with a "Her family says" callout, glossary updated with a family note.

  glossary flipped to make Pani the primary entry, MISSION.md now lists
  family-confirmed forms. Lesson 3's warm-up and quiz both drill pani to

## Vocabulary introduced

| Phonetic | Meaning | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| bhalo cholche | it's going well | answer to kaj kemon cholche |
| ami khub byasto | I'm very busy | pairs with bhalo cholche |
| shobai bhalo ache | everyone is well | answer re: your family |
| alhamdulillah | praise God / all is well | most natural answer in a Muslim household |
| ektu ektu shikhchi | I'm learning little by little | the charmer — answer to "Bangla shikhchen?" |
| ar apni? / ar tumi? | and you? (formal / familiar) | conversation multiplier + register switch |
| ami ...-e kaj kori | I work in/at ... | English job title inside Bangla is normal |
| kaj koren / kaj koro | (you) work (apni/tumi) | -en → -o register shift |
| shikhchen / shikhcho | (you) are learning (apni/tumi) | -chen → -cho register shift |
